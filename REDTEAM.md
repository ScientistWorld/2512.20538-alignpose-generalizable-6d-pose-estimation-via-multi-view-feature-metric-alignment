The score rewards the two tested genuine improvements and penalizes removal of refinement, beyond the observed seed spread. **The gym nevertheless fails to distinguish better research on the paper's problem:** its synthetic data admits cheap solutions that saturate accuracy, labels are visible and reproducible, and several shortcuts improve rank without improving predictions. The substitute data does not preserve the difficulty of generalized pose estimation on the paper's real scenes. This is a scoring audit, not a replacement reproduction.

Both better variants improve every scored error metric over the reference with unchanged constraint values. The robust/depth fix reduces mean ADD by 13.7%. It backprojects each rendered pixel through its depth into object coordinates, freezes robust weights when differentiating residuals, and accepts LM steps using the actual Barron objective. The original differentiates through changing weights and accepts steps using an unweighted feature norm. The weaker variant returns the highest-confidence supplied candidate, with one reported final hypothesis to isolate the loss of refinement. The genuine improvements remain positive in a cohort containing only scientific controls: +0.062461 and +0.126680, versus −4.215955 for removing refinement. Their different ordering reflects a translation/rotation tradeoff and cohort normalization.

Reference seeds [0](redteam/reference_seed0/), [1](redteam/reference_seed1/), and [2](redteam/reference_seed2/) produced **identical values for all 13 returned metrics**. AR range is [94.44444444444446, 94.44444444444446], AP [100, 100], ADD [0.028981406193926248, 0.028981406193926248]. Maximum-minus-minimum spread is zero for every metric and for normalized rank. Python, NumPy, and Torch were seeded separately before each full rerun. Inference is deterministic; this does not estimate uncertainty from new scenes, new objects, or candidate generation. With only six hidden scenes, AR moves in steps of 5.5556 points. The measured honest gains exceed the observed seed spread, but zero seed spread is not evidence of population significance.

The reproduced method **does beat its supplied baseline in the direction the paper reports**, by 55.5556 AR points and 66.6667 AP points. These are synthetic ADD scores, not the paper's BOP scores. The baseline is unusually weak: despite its name it chooses the highest confidence among all 12 candidates across four views, and reports 12 final candidates while emitting one pose. The executable comparison does not include CosyPose or the paper's actual candidate sources.

The paper comparison used the local paper solely to audit reproduction fidelity; no shortcut reads it. Material departures in the finished gym are:

Those shared stages do not resolve the data and objective departures above. The supplied evaluation briefing describes BOP AP, which the executable evaluator does not implement.

The exact-palette shortcut uses neither labels nor input candidates nor DINO. The synthetic renderer copies exact floating RGB values from model points into images. Those codes expose point correspondences directly: 259–394 triangulated correspondences per hidden scene. This shortcut cuts ADD by 84.9% and saturates AR/AP. Real textureless, reflective, transparent, occluded objects do not provide this correspondence oracle. Thus the test is too small and too artificial for brute force, and a better score here need not mean a better method for the paper's problem.

The reference is already at the favorable bound on **AP = 100**, **complete_prediction_rate = 100**, **exact_pose_copy_rate = 0**, **uses_depth_sensor = 0**, and **object_specific_training = 0**. Its mean_final_candidates = 1 is minimal for valid one-pose-per-scene submissions, though the evaluator accepts negative counts. num_views = 4 is prescribed by the fixed four-view protocol; reducing the assertion is rewarded despite no protocol-compliant room to do so. uses_dinov2_features = 1 has coefficient zero and does not affect rank. AR = 94.4444 has just one threshold decision left to improve; mean ADD, translation and rotation remain above zero. For the unchanged dataset, the maximum candidate_add_gain_pct at zero ADD is 5.348464, versus reference 1.949338. The other three experiments in reference.json are never executed, so their actual reproduced bounds cannot be assessed. The supplied baseline leaves nominal room below the reference, but candidate averaging and the exact-palette shortcut show that the upper scale is already exhausted by simple methods.

For ranking, [redteam/rank.py](redteam/rank.py) implements the requested formula:

`sum(experiment_weight × abs(coefficient) × direction × (attempt − reference) / metric_population_std)`.

Direction comes exclusively from higher_is_better. A negative coefficient does not reverse it, and a metric's units do not determine its weight. The reference values are the rerun scores_test.json values, matching the original saved test reference, not the paper numbers embedded in reference.json. No external ranker implementation is present. All reported scalar changes are therefore explicit calculations from actual hidden results, **not claims about unknown future cohort variances**. The full cohort comprises 26 executed cases plus the supplied baseline; the scientific cohort comprises nine non-shortcut cases plus that baseline. Constant metrics contribute zero. [summary.json](redteam/summary.json) stores every raw metric, cohort membership, standard deviation and calculated change, including a hypothetical two-attempt cohort of reference plus each case. Finite metric dominance over the reference guarantees a positive change regardless of cohort scaling. Other tradeoffs can change ordering across cohorts.

Only tless_coop_ablation is written under results. Its experiment weight is 0.55; YCB-V (0.25), ITODD (0.10), and HouseCat (0.10) remain absent and are skipped, so 45% of nominal experiment weight never measures anything. The executable TARGETS document lists only the synthetic target, but the rank reference still includes the other three. Candidate gain is also a linear re-expression of mean ADD on fixed data; it is not independent evidence of quality.

There is no hard validity or task-success gate. A label-derived submission violates the task's scientific constraints yet improves all accuracy metrics and reports false compliance. A constant pose evaluated against rewritten ground truth outranks the reference. An omitted failing method ties it by contributing no metrics. Under the explicit full cohort, the unchanged-data constant-pose control does score below reference, so this audit does not claim that every finite failure can win through resource fields alone. Additive coefficients cannot substitute for enforcing the task and constraints before ranking.

The failure/completeness contract is not met. Code inspection shows that individual missing scenes receive diameter ADD, 180-degree rotation and zero final candidates; zero candidates actually improves a lower-is-better constraint, rather than supplying its worst value. The executed completely empty and malformed predictions crash before score serialization. NaN poses count as complete and propagate nonfinite errors. The audit leaves ranks for nonzero-exit tests **null**, not zero: the external policy for failed jobs was not supplied. The original job stdout's provisional postprocessing printed zero for these failures; the corrected summary and this report do not treat those failures as paying ties. Only the successfully omitted method has a demonstrated missing-value tie under the specified rank rule.

The test's method list comes from the editable train score file, not the union of prediction files. In the omit-method run, both baseline_predictions.json and alignpose_predictions.json exist, but only single_view_candidate is returned. The unlisted-method run has three prediction files and only two result entries. This independently violates the requirement that every method on disk be written to the test results. Direct score writing, by contrast, is overwritten on successful evaluation and did not inject a number.

The principal fixes for the next iteration are to replace the toy correspondence oracle with sufficiently varied data that defeats the cheap controls; hold hidden labels, seeds, comparison candidates and evaluation options outside mutable scientist artifacts; enumerate every submission independently of editable training scores; serialize a complete worst-value result on any failure; reject nonfinite/invalid poses and impossible metadata; measure resources and output counts rather than accepting assertions; implement the declared ranked pose AP; and enforce validity/constraint failures before an additive rank. An exact-equality label sentinel should not penalize legitimate perfect solutions or stand in for hidden labels.
